PROJECT 03 · OFFLINE RAG LEARNING ASSISTANT 2025

PyMentor — RAG-Based AI Learning Assistant

An offline, privacy-first tutoring application that transforms lecture videos into conversational, source-grounded study material without cloud subscriptions or external APIs.

  • Zero-Cloud Whisper Audio Pipeline: Transcribes lecture audio via Whisper and computes semantic embeddings with BGE-M3 into a local vector store.
